# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Community builders and hobbyist organizers — people running open-source projects, interest groups, online classrooms, newsletter communities, or private circles. They're evaluating Sabha as an alternative to Discord or Slack. They arrive skeptical, impatient with SaaS upsells, and want to know instantly whether this is trustworthy and worth trying. Technical enough to care about "open source" and "self-hosted"; non-technical enough to need 30-second setup.

## Product Purpose

Sabha is an open-source community chat platform: rooms, threads, DMs, search. Free on sabha.co (shared SaaS), managed-hosting via Sabha Cloud, or self-hosted MIT licensed. The landing page's job is to convert visitors who are fed up with Discord/Slack into community creators — either by clicking "Create your community" or by exploring GitHub/self-hosting.

## Brand Personality

Warm, simple, reliable. Not corporate, not cute — opinionated without being rigid. The voice is direct and confident ("Yep."), technical without being nerdy, self-assured without being smug. Think: a well-made tool you trust, built by people who care about the craft.

## Anti-references

- Discord: feature bloat, upsell ecosystem, dark gamer aesthetic, noise
- Generic SaaS dashboards: blue gradients, hero metrics, buzzword copy
- Enterprise tools (Mattermost, Rocket.Chat): complexity theater, Kubernetes vibes
- Cream/sand/warm-neutral body backgrounds — the saturated AI default

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint is the brand** — personality from thoughtful defaults and subtle touches, not decoration
2. **Content first** — the conversation is the hero; UI chrome recedes
3. **Clarity over cleverness** — no mystery meat, no hidden features, immediately understandable
4. **Honest copy** — specific nouns and verbs describing what the product literally does; no buzzwords
5. **Accessible by default** — WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader support

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Focus visible states required. Keyboard navigable. Reduced motion respected (already present in CSS).
